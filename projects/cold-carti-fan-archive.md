> `[TEAM COURSEWORK]` · Responsive front-end website · Two-person SE-2501 course project

**Live website:** [kanatovs.github.io/music-bandv3](https://kanatovs.github.io/music-bandv3/)

## Product

An unofficial, non-commercial fan archive for Cold Carti. The site lets visitors browse releases, filter the gallery by year, rate covers, build a temporary personal collection, and use a local two-step contact-form demo.

## What the site includes

- Responsive pages for releases, tour cards, project information, contact, and studio/gallery views.
- Cover gallery with year filtering, selected-cover details, rating controls, and a browser-session collection.
- Client-side interaction built with DOM updates, arrays, filters, callbacks, keyboard navigation, and reduced-motion support.
- Two-step contact form with local required-field, e-mail, and password validation.
- Local Bootstrap assets and no backend/API dependency.

## Tech

`HTML` · `CSS` · `JavaScript` · local `Bootstrap`
